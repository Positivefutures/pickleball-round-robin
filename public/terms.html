What the app is

It builds round robin schedules. You give it your players, how many courts you have and how many rounds you want, and it works out who plays with whom and who sits out. It keeps that group so you can run it again next week.

It is a scheduling aid and nothing else. It does not book courts, take payments from your players, settle an argument about a score, or know anything about the people on your list beyond what you typed.

Using it, with or without an account

No account is needed. Most people never make one, and everything works that way, on your own device.

An account exists for one reason, which is to copy your groups to your other phone or tablet. It uses your email address and no password, so the sign-in codes that arrive in your inbox are what protects it. Do not pass one on to anybody, and keep the address one you can still open.

The names you type in belong to other people

This is the part most worth reading. If you run a group, the list is your players' names, not yours, and you are the one who decided to put them in. That makes it your responsibility rather than mine.

  • Type in what a game needs. A first name and a rating is usually the whole of it.
  • Do not put anything else about a person in there. Not a phone number, not an address, not anything about their health, and not a private remark about how they play.
  • Tell your players the list exists if it would matter to them.
